# Changelog

Newer versions first. Saved setups from older versions always load in newer ones; where something was removed, the
setup is moved to the closest thing that is left.

## 0.14
- The **MINI POPS** kit now plays real samples of a Korg Mini Pops 120 (Drumkito's Korg Mini Pops Sample Pack). Its
  CLAP and PERC rows play a bright snare (SNAP) and a wood block (WOOD).
- New logo: the loading screen's half glass of chocolate and strawberry milk.

## 0.13
- Real percussion instead of beeps on the PERC row: the 808 has a high tom, the 606 a shaker, the CR-78 a conga, the
  MINI POPS maracas and LO-FI a low tom.

## 0.12
- The **LM-1** kit is back, playing real samples of a Linn LM-1 (rossf's pack on Freesound). Its CLAP and PERC rows
  play the low and high toms.

## 0.11
- The HIP-HOP and 909 kits were removed (a setup on HIP-HOP moves to LO-FI, one on the 909 to ROCK).

## 0.10
- New defaults: CHOC plays PIANO and BERRY plays MUSIC BOX, both in SINGLE KEY mode, both instruments and the drums
  at volume 70, the 808 kit, 110 BPM.
- The synthesised LM-1 kit was removed (a setup on it moves to the 808).

## 0.9.x
- **Latch**: press REC while holding notes to keep them playing. Latched notes keep playing when the split moves,
  and their keys are free to play again straight away.
- The OCTAVE mode (a note and the note an octave above) replaces CHORD IN KEY (a setup on it moves to CHORD MAJOR).
- PADDOCK can hold chords.
- Three new sounds: BUBBLE POP (lead), CLOUD (pad) and CURDLE (bass).
- Both instruments start in SINGLE KEY mode.

## 0.8
- The drum pattern and PADDOCK start empty; PADDOCK plays pad sounds only, and a new note starts at C3.
- Holding REC on the main screen resets everything (the save slots are kept).
- The FUZZ and FM BASS sounds were removed (setups on them move to SYNTH BASS); the basses sound an octave higher.
- The loading screen shows the name bigger.

## 0.7
- The SEQ page has two tabs: DAIRY DRUMS (the drum machine) and PADDOCK (a pad sequencer).
- The loading screen fills a glass with milk.

## 0.6
- Effects for each instrument: reverb, delay, distortion, chorus and phaser.
- The ARP page (rate, octaves, gate, notes) and the wave editor (GLO).
- Holding REC on an option page resets it.
- The save page: 4 slots for the whole setup.
- 0.6.1: two arpeggiated MUSIC BOXes could overload the FM-1 and break up; the sound engine is lighter now, and an
  overload guard keeps the sound clean. The drum page shows the CPU load.

## 0.5
- 23 sounds (7 were removed), new cow-print headers and a new loading screen.

## 0.4
- OCT- and OCT+ choose the instrument with a tap (no need to hold).

## 0.3
- SELECT moves the split, PRESETS changes the key, ALGORITHM sets the tempo.

## 0.2
- The milk look: CHOC and BERRY, cream screens, a glass for each volume.

## 0.1
- The first version: two instruments on a split keyboard, modes, a split screen and a 16-step drum machine.
